Help & Info

About

Web News Feed by Mattias

A browser-based RSS news feed reader. Add your favorite RSS feeds, organize them into rows, and read the latest headlines - all from your browser.

Features

Feed Management
  • Add feeds - provide a name, RSS URL, row (1-10), and order (1-10).
  • Amalgamated feeds - combine multiple RSS URLs (comma-separated) into one category.
  • Edit & Remove - modify or delete feeds from the Manage Feeds dialog.
  • Edit Feed shortcut - jump straight to editing the currently active feed without opening Manage Feeds first.
  • Rows 1-10 - organize feed buttons into horizontal rows.
  • Order 1-10 - controls the position of a feed within its row. Max 10 feeds per row.
  • Swap on conflict - editing a feed into an occupied row/order slot swaps the two feeds.
Reading
  • Pagination - articles are shown 12 per page, up to 10 pages per feed.
  • Refresh button (↻) - re-fetches every feed right now, ignoring the cache. It is unavailable while any feed is loading.
  • Auto-refresh - a feed refreshes in the background only once its articles are older than 15 minutes, and never more than once per 15 minutes (clicking a feed, returning to the tab and the timer all follow this rule).
  • Caching - feeds open instantly from the cache. While a feed is being fetched you see "Fetching news..." instead of its old articles; a feed that failed shows a note instead of being re-searched on every click.
  • Search - filter and highlight articles by keyword in real time.
  • Click headlines - opens the full article in a new tab.
  • Source badges - every article shows its source domain.
Data Config
  • Access - located in the site footer directly above the GitHub Repository link.
  • Export Config - download your feeds and settings as a JSON file.
  • Import Config - Replaces your current feeds and settings with a previously exported config file. You are asked to confirm before choosing a file.
  • Reset Config - Permanently erases every feed and setting in this browser and restores the defaults. Requires typing RESET to confirm.
Appearance
  • Dark / Light mode - toggle theme with the button.
  • Responsive - works on desktop and mobile.

Keyboard Shortcuts & Navigation

  • Enter in search box - triggers search.
  • Escape or click background - closes any open modal.

Technical Notes

RSS feeds are fetched via CORS proxies since browsers block direct cross-origin requests. All configuration is stored in your browser's localStorage - nothing is sent to a server.
